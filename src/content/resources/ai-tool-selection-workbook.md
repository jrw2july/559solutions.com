## Preliminary outline

The planned workbook will help a team compare tools based on:

- actual need;
- users and workflow fit;
- data sensitivity;
- integration requirements;
- learning curve;
- price and ongoing maintenance;
- ownership;
- measurable outcomes.

This outline is not the final workbook. Product files, final price, support terms, and a checkout destination still require owner approval.
