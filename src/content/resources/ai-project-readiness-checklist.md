## Define the work before the technology

These ten questions create a practical starting point for a project conversation:

1. What decision must this project improve?
2. Who owns that decision?
3. What problem exists without the proposed technology?
4. What information is required?
5. Where does that information come from?
6. What must remain human judgment?
7. What risks or constraints matter?
8. How will success be measured?
9. What is the smallest useful version?
10. Who will maintain the result?
